FAQ's

Have Any Questions For Us?

Are the cosmetics cruelty-free and vegan?
Yes, 100%! None of our ingredients or products are ever tested on animals, and all formulas are free from animal-derived components.
How long does the makeup stay on?
Our foundations and tints are designed for 12 to 16 hours of sweat-resistant, crease-proof wear without oxidizing.
Do you offer shade matching assistance?
Yes, our online virtual shade finder and beauty consultants are available 24/7 to help you select your ideal undertone and match.
Are your products safe for sensitive skin?
All formulations are hypoallergenic, non-comedogenic, and rigorously tested under dermatological supervision.
What is the shelf life of your cosmetics?
Unopened products have a shelf life of up to 3 years. Once opened, we recommend using within 12 to 18 months for peak freshness.
